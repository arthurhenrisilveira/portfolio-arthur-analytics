# Customer Retention Cohort & RFM - Olist

## Context

Retention analytics project using the Olist dataset to analyze customer behavior, repeat purchasing, and segmentation opportunities.

## Business problem

Identify how customer retention evolves over time and how customer groups can be prioritized based on purchasing behavior.

## Analytical approach

- Built cohort views to analyze retention patterns.
- Applied RFM segmentation to group customers by recency, frequency, and monetary value.
- Organized insights for dashboard and business reporting use.
- Connected analytical outputs to retention and customer lifecycle questions.

## Tools

- Power BI
- Python
- pandas
- Excel
- Cohort analysis
- RFM segmentation

## Outputs

- Cohort analysis structure
- RFM customer segments
- Retention indicators
- Dashboard-ready business insights

## Business relevance

The project supports customer retention strategy, lifecycle prioritization, and commercial decision-making based on customer behavior.
