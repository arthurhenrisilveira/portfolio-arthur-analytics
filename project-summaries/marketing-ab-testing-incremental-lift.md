# Marketing A/B Testing & Incremental Lift

## Context

Growth analytics project focused on campaign experimentation, A/B testing, and incremental lift measurement.

## Business problem

Marketing teams need to understand whether campaign results represent true incremental impact or only observed performance differences.

## Analytical approach

- Compared control and treatment groups.
- Applied A/B testing logic to evaluate performance differences.
- Estimated incremental lift from campaign exposure.
- Translated results into campaign analysis and decision-making implications.

## Tools

- Python
- pandas
- Excel
- A/B testing
- Incremental lift analysis
- KPI reporting

## Outputs

- Experiment analysis structure
- Incremental lift calculation
- Campaign performance interpretation
- Business recommendations for growth decisions

## Business relevance

The project supports clearer marketing investment decisions by distinguishing correlation, observed movement, and incremental campaign impact.
