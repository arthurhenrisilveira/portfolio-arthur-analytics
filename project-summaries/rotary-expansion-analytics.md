# Rotary Expansion Analytics

## Context

An anonymized portfolio case focused on expansion analytics, territorial analysis, executive reporting, and prioritization.

## Business problem

Decision-makers need a clear way to compare expansion opportunities across territories while protecting confidential Rotary information.

## Analytical approach

- Organized available indicators into a structured diagnostic.
- Compared territories through prioritization criteria.
- Translated analytical findings into dashboard-ready views and executive recommendations.
- Kept the case anonymized and focused on method, not sensitive operational details.

## Tools

- Python
- pandas
- Excel
- Dashboards
- Executive reporting

## Outputs

- Territorial analysis structure
- Prioritization framework
- Executive summary logic
- Dashboard-oriented indicators

## Business relevance

The project shows how analytics can support expansion decisions, align stakeholders, and communicate priorities in a concise, business-readable format.
